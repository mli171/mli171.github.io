Research

Statistical methods for complex data

My research develops computational and statistical methodology for data with dependence, structural changes, and heterogeneous measurement characteristics.

Computational statistics and optimization

Genetic-algorithm methods for changepoint detection and statistical model selection, with emphasis on reliable search, practical computation, and reproducible R software.

Related work: changepointGA, GAReg, structured and consensus-guided island-model genetic algorithms.

Categorical and dependent time series

Statistical estimation and inference for autocorrelated ordered categorical observations and related dependent data.

Related work: changepoint analysis in ordinal categorical series, conditional least squares for autoregressive ordered probit models.

Computational biostatistics

Methods for microbiome and T-cell receptor sequencing data, including compositional modeling, heterogeneous sequencing depth, and diversity analysis.

Related work: multi-bin rarefaction, CAFT, mixed-effect kernel methods for alpha diversity.

Interdisciplinary applications

Collaborations span biomedical research, climate and environmental data, and agricultural systems. My methodological work aims to create tools that can be applied across scientific contexts.
